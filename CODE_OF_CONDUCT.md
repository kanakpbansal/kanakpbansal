# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, gender, background, experience level, or identity.

We pledge to act professionally, respectfully, and collaboratively when participating in this project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, inappropriate comments, or any other conduct that would make the project environment unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly and appropriately.

Project maintainers will make every reasonable effort to protect the privacy and safety of anyone reporting an incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will consider the circumstances and severity of each violation when determining appropriate action. Possible responses may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restriction of participation, or permanent removal from project activities.

Maintainers will communicate enforcement decisions clearly and consistently while respecting the privacy of those involved.
